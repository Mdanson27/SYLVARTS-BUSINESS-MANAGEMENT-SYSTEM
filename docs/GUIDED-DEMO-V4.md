# Guided Demo V4

The guided demo is designed so a presenter can simply tell a viewer:

> Tap **Guided Demo** and follow the walkthrough.

## Where the tour is visible

- On the login screen as **Take the Guided Demo**
- On the dashboard as a prominent guided-demo banner
- As a floating **Guided Demo** button throughout the logged-in system
- In the sidebar Presentation Mode panel

## Tour modes

### Full system tour

Automatically opens and explains all 12 major sections:

1. Dashboard
2. Point of Sale
3. Inventory
4. Customers
5. Suppliers
6. Purchasing
7. Receipts
8. Invoices
9. Expenses
10. Tracking
11. Reports
12. Admin & Roles

Each section explains:

- What the module does
- Why it matters to the business
- How it connects to other modules
- What the presenter should try while demonstrating it

### Explore Sections

The tour hub shows all 12 sections as cards. Tap any card to jump directly to that module and its explanation.

## Presenter controls

During the walkthrough the viewer can:

- Go Back
- Go to the Next Section
- Jump to any section from the section navigator
- Pause & explore the current section
- Resume from the floating resume control
- End the tour
- Restart after finishing

## Recommended live presentation story

1. Start the Guided Demo from the login screen.
2. Explain the Dashboard command centre.
3. Move to POS and demonstrate selling an exact size/colour variant.
4. Pause the tour and complete a sample sale.
5. Resume and show Inventory to demonstrate the stock reduction.
6. Show Purchasing to explain reorder recommendations and stock receiving.
7. Show Receipts and Invoices as transaction documentation.
8. Show Expenses and Tracking to explain operational control.
9. Finish in Reports with Revenue → COGS → Gross Profit → Expenses → Estimated Net.
10. Finish in Admin & Roles and preview Manager and Sales Staff permissions.

The full tour uses the Owner / Admin preview so every module can be demonstrated.
